FAQ

Answers before you book

Do you provide packing material?

Yes. We can provide cartons, bubble wrap, stretch film, labels, tape, and protective covers based on the move type.

Can I book same-city shifting on short notice?

In many cases, yes. Availability depends on crew and vehicle slots for your selected moving date.

Do you handle intercity relocation?

Yes. Move It supports city-to-city relocation with planned pickup, safe transport, and delivery coordination.

How is the estimate calculated?

We consider distance, home size, item volume, packing needs, floors, lift access, vehicle size, and manpower.
